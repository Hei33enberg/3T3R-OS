## Names of forks

If you fork this repository, pick a name that does not contain "33.0S" or "3T3R". For example:

- `frame-fork-by-acme` — fine.
- `33.0S-pro`, `3t3r-os-cloud`, `3T3R-enterprise` — suggests official affiliation. Ask us first.
- `33.0S` or `3T3R` under another organisation, package scope or registry — please don't.

## Why this matters

A name tells people what is official. A look-alike that borrows our names misleads the people who rely on it. The code stays free under Apache-2.0; the names stay with us.
